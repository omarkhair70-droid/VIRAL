# Product Principles — Season 2

## 1) Practical Before Decorative
- **Explanation:** People come to solve a real item problem first.
- **Product implication:** Flows, CTAs, and decision points must be clearer than visual effects.
- **Example:** Publish flow prioritizes item quality, condition, and wanted swap over optional styling.

## 2) Warm but Not Childish
- **Explanation:** The app should feel human and local without feeling unserious.
- **Product implication:** Copy uses simple Egyptian Arabic with calm confidence.
- **Example:** "اتفقوا بهدوء" is preferred over hype-heavy or playful slang.

## 3) Trust Is Part of the Product, Not a Separate Page
- **Explanation:** Safety cannot live only in one help page.
- **Product implication:** Trust signals appear in profile, item detail, offers, and deal room.
- **Example:** Deal room shows reminders and clear participant context before completion.

## 4) Every Item Can Have a Story
- **Explanation:** Context can increase relevance and perceived value.
- **Product implication:** Story fields are encouraged in publish and visible in item detail.
- **Example:** "الكرسي ده من أول مكتب اشتغلت عليه" adds meaning beyond category and condition.

## 5) Every Screen Should Make the Next Step Obvious
- **Explanation:** Confusion kills swap completion.
- **Product implication:** Each major screen needs one clear primary CTA and secondary fallback actions.
- **Example:** Item detail makes "ابعت عرض" obvious before less-critical actions.

## 6) Swapping Should Feel Calm and Organized
- **Explanation:** Swaps involve two people and uncertainty; chaos reduces trust.
- **Product implication:** Deal state, messages, and checklists should guide users step by step.
- **Example:** Deal room checklist clarifies what is done and what remains before marking complete.

## 7) Premium Means Clean, Clear, and Trustworthy — Not Complicated
- **Explanation:** Premium quality is clarity and confidence, not feature overload.
- **Product implication:** Remove noise, tighten hierarchy, improve readability and spacing.
- **Example:** Profile 2.0 emphasizes trust summary, completed swaps, and review quality.

## 8) No Fake Urgency
- **Explanation:** Pressure tactics can increase bad decisions and reduce trust.
- **Product implication:** Avoid countdown-like manipulation and panic messaging.
- **Example:** Use honest availability notes instead of "last chance" hype language.

## 9) No Public Safety Overpromising
- **Explanation:** Real safety is layered and imperfect.
- **Product implication:** Copy must avoid guarantees and explain practical precautions.
- **Example:** Never claim "آمن 100%"; provide reminders and reporting paths.

## 10) Build for Real Completed Swaps, Not Vanity Metrics
- **Explanation:** Meaningful success is successful exchange quality, not empty activity.
- **Product implication:** Prioritize completion flow, reviews, and actionable feedback loops.
- **Example:** A feature is successful when it improves completed trusted swaps and review usefulness.
