# TESWA Season 5 — Final Audit (Phase 60)

## 1) Season 5 purpose
Season 5 reset Teswa from a marketplace-first framing into a possibility-first product: people open a door for things that left their lives, and value evolves through curiosity, proposals, stories, and swaps.

## 2) What changed across Phases 52–59
- **Home:** shifted toward possibility feed and curiosity-first discovery.
- **Explore:** rebuilt beyond marketplace-search framing.
- **Publish:** reframed around opening a door for a thing and value intent.
- **Thing Detail:** aligned card and value-question semantics.
- **Offer flow:** reframed as proposal-based interaction (اقتراحات).
- **Public Motion:** established visible value movement layer.
- **People:** framed users as exchange personalities, not listings accounts.

## 3) What Phase 60 integrated and cleaned
- Final consistency pass across navigation, dashboard/account, notifications, and static explanatory pages.
- Replaced residual market-era surface wording where it conflicted with Season 5 decisions.
- Resolved known copy duplication in the offer composer inline desire block.

## 4) Canonical vocabulary decisions
- **Explore, not Market** for `/items` user-facing navigation.
- **Proposal (اقتراح), not generic Offer (عرض)** in reset user-facing account/system surfaces.
- **Thing / Possibility / Open Door** language where intent semantics are explained.
- **Public Motion / حركة القيمة** for `/drops` framing.

## 5) Technical audit checklist and results
- **Broken route check (`/feed`):** no live code references found.
- **Invalid legacy token/class check:** no active regressions found for `rounded-card`, `rounded-panel`, `type-label`, `p-panel-xl`, `type-display`, `type-lead`, `type-headline`.
- **Scoped vocabulary drift check:** high-surface audited files were aligned to Season 5 vocabulary decisions.

## 6) Intentionally deferred after Season 5
- Broader release and Play Store preparation tracks.
- Dedicated performance and expanded QA passes (if planned as separate tracks).
- Public completed-swap storytelling with explicit consent model.
- Future recommendation/matching systems.

## 7) Season 5 completion verdict
Season 5 is complete from a product-language and concept-integration perspective. The product now presents one coherent doctrine across navigation, exploration, publishing, proposals, public motion, and account/system communication surfaces.
