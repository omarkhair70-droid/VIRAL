# Story Swap Strategy — Season 2

## A) Why Story Matters
- Stories make items feel real.
- Stories raise perceived value.
- Stories help trust.
- Stories differentiate Baddelha from OLX/Facebook patterns.

## B) Story Fields Later
- Item story
- Why I’m swapping it
- Who it’s good for
- Memory/context
- What I want instead

## C) Examples
- "الكاميرا دي استخدمتها في أول مشروع تصوير ليا."
- "الجاكيت ده كان معايا في حفلة مهمة."
- "الكتاب ده عليه ملاحظاتي."
- "الكرسي ده من أول مكتب اشتغلت عليه."

## D) Story Rules
- Story should be optional but encouraged.
- Not every item needs dramatic story.
- Avoid fake luxury.
- Avoid overlong storytelling.
- Keep it useful and honest.

## E) UI Implications
- Publish flow needs story field.
- Item detail needs story section.
- Profile should show story-rich items.
- Drops should highlight strongest stories.


## F) Phase 29 implementation update (May 12, 2026)
- Story capture is now productized inside publish flow via optional guided fields.
- Publish preview now includes story content before final submit.
- Deeper story curation/presentation remains scheduled for later phases (especially Phase 30+).


## Phase 33 status update
Phase 33 added the story label and curated discovery layer.
Drops now manually highlight the strongest story-rich items.
