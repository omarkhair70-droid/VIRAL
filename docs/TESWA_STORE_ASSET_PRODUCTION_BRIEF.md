# TESWA Store Asset Production Brief (GP-05)

## 1) Google Play required assets

### App icon (required)
- Size: **512 × 512 px**
- Format: **PNG**
- File size: **max 1024 KB**

### Feature graphic (required)
- Size: **1024 × 500 px**
- Format: **JPEG** or **24-bit PNG**
- Constraint: **No alpha channel**

### Phone screenshots
- **Minimum required to publish:** 2
- **Recommended for stronger merchandising eligibility:** at least 4 high-quality portrait screenshots at **1080 × 1920** or higher
- Keep real UI central and readable.
- Avoid status-bar clutter (notifications, carrier clutter, random service icons) where possible.
- Add headline overlays only if needed, and keep them secondary to product UI.

## 2) Teswa screenshot storyboard (recommended 6 portrait shots)

### 1) Home / Possibility Feed
- **Overlay headline:** كل حاجة ممكن تبدأ من هنا
- **Route/surface to capture:** Home feed (`/`) with active listing cards.
- **Should be visible:** mixture of real-looking posts, clear card hierarchy, navigation clarity.
- **Avoid:** empty-state-only view, debug labels, placeholder-only content.

### 2) Explore / discovery worlds
- **Overlay headline:** استكشف الاحتمالات بطريقتك
- **Route/surface to capture:** Explore/discovery surface (current production explore route).
- **Should be visible:** filtering or discovery structure, variety in visible content.
- **Avoid:** repetitive cards, unfinished taxonomy labels.

### 3) Publish flow
- **Overlay headline:** افتح للحاجة باب قيمة جديد
- **Route/surface to capture:** Publish/create listing flow.
- **Should be visible:** clear form structure (photos/title/details/category/condition).
- **Avoid:** validation errors, partially broken draft state.

### 4) Thing detail / Value Question Page
- **Overlay headline:** إنت شايف دي تِسوى إيه؟
- **Route/surface to capture:** Listing detail/value-question page.
- **Should be visible:** item visuals, description, value context, action area.
- **Avoid:** personal data exposure, cropped critical UI areas.

### 5) Offer composer
- **Overlay headline:** قدّم اقتراحًا له معنى
- **Route/surface to capture:** Proposal/offer composer surface.
- **Should be visible:** message/proposal fields and clear call-to-action flow.
- **Avoid:** sensitive identifiers, noisy keyboard obstruction if possible.

### 6) Public Motion or People
- **Overlay headline candidate A:** شوف حركة القيمة
- **Overlay headline candidate B:** تعرف على أسلوب كل شخص
- **Route/surface to capture:** choose stronger visual between Public Motion stream and People/profile discovery.
- **Should be visible:** trust/social signal surface (activity, profiles, or evaluation cues).
- **Avoid:** weak-contrast capture, sparse data snapshot.

## 3) Feature graphic creative direction
- Warm editorial background aligned with Teswa palette.
- Main wordmark: **تِسوى**
- One-line copy: **حاجتك ممكن تِسوى إيه عند غيرك؟**
- Visual style: minimal, premium, uncluttered.
- Do **not** include Google Play badge.
- Do **not** include ranking/award claims.
- Do **not** include misleading mockups or non-product promises.

## 4) Asset-production next step after GP-05
- Screenshots and feature graphic should be produced manually by design/marketing workflow (or approved creative tooling).
- Final exported files must be validated against exact Google Play dimensions/specs before upload.
